Wireframe Studio

Build looping geometric motion — tweak, preview, export.

Quick start

Pick a scene at the top, choose a Look to get a starting point, then shape it with the sliders on the right. The preview loops live. When it looks right, open Export and pick a format.

Keyboard

  • SpacePlay / pause
  • RRandomize the current scene
  • 123Switch scene
  • ←→Step through the loop (when paused)
  • ?Open this help

About export

Every format renders exactly one loop at your chosen resolution, so the result tiles seamlessly. WebM is the quickest for video; PNG sequence is best for editing software; GIF is for quick previews; HTML gives you a standalone player file.
